{# Login animation: the pomegranate falls to a line and bursts. Decorative, so `aria-hidden` and `inert` -- there is nothing here to read and nothing to focus. A screen reader lands on the page itself. Pure CSS, and that is the important part: the overlay ends at `opacity: 0; visibility: hidden` through `animation-fill-mode: forwards`, so it clears itself even if no JavaScript ever runs. A welcome screen that can wedge shut over the app is worse than no welcome screen. app.js only removes the dead node afterwards. The twelve scatter vectors live in theme.css (`.splash__burst i:nth-child`), not in style attributes here, so a `style-src` without 'unsafe-inline' can cover this page. They are written out rather than generated. Two reasons. A `random()` occasionally throws every aril the same way and the one bad roll is the one a user sees. And the first hand-written set was near mirror-symmetric, which read as a dome rather than a burst -- so these are deliberately uneven, and weighted low and wide along the line, the way a fruit opens on the ground rather than the way a firework goes up. #}
